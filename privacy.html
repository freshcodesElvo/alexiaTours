Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

At Alexia's Tours, we respect your data storage space and are dedicated to managing your identifiable records safely and transparently. This compliance document highlights how we track, process, and secure metrics transmitted across our travel booking pipelines.

1. Information Captured in Transit

When interacting with our client web layouts or forms, we collect information necessary to fulfill your travel arrangements, including:

  • Identity keys (Full name, contact phone rows, email endpoints).
  • Travel logistics data (Passport parameters where required, specific destination selections).
  • Automated analytical tracking metrics (IP locations, processing cookies via browser contexts).

2. Processing Architecture Paths

Your records are processed strictly to ensure smooth execution contexts across your trips, specifically for:

  • Confirming tour package allocations and database reservations cleanly.
  • Answering service inquiries via our system channels.
  • Pushing critical notification updates regarding modified itinerary timelines.

3. Asset Infrastructure Security

We deploy modern encryption protocols to prevent unauthorized file tracking or payload leaks. Your payment transitions and inquiry data strings are safely routed through secure SSL tunnels straight to our hosting repositories.

4. Third-Party Resource Transfers

Alexia's Tours will never sell or trade your data profiles. We pass data parameters to trusted destination entities (such as localized hotels, field guides, or transit authorities) solely to fulfill your booked tour requirements.

5. Your Rights

You can request to audit, edit, or purge your records from our cloud server storage configurations at any time. Simply drop an email to our team endpoints to clear your profile logs.
