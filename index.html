Essential Travel Guide

Everything you need to know before you fly to Kenya with Alexia's Tours.

Entry & Visa

Kenya is now visa-free, but you must apply for an Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA) at least 3 days before travel.

Apply Here

Packing Essentials

  • Light cotton clothing (neutral colors)
  • High SPF sunscreen & sunglasses
  • Type G power adapters (UK style)
  • Binoculars for wildlife viewing

Health & Safety

Consult your doctor for Malaria prophylaxis. We recommend drinking only bottled water, which we provide during all game drives.

Money Matters

The local currency is the Kenya Shilling (KES). Major credit cards and M-Pesa are widely accepted in cities.

Weather & Timing

June to October is the "Great Migration" season. January to March is excellent for bird watching and clear skies.
